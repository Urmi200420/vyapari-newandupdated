## 3. Main gaps, in order of impact

1. **No orders in the backend.** Checkout, tracking, the seller's order screen, admin assignment, the courier's trips, the delivery OTP and the wallets all run on browser demo data. This blocks the SRS "dashboard interaction loop" and the real 4-digit delivery OTP.
2. **Customer login bypass** (`BYPASS_LOGIN`, `VITE_REAL_AUTH=false`): guests aren't sent to login, and the customer dashboard isn't tied to the logged-in account.
3. Customer write-a-review and real password change are missing.
4. Delivery registration lacks bank details and a Three-Wheeler option; duty status isn't in the database.
5. The catalogue isn't in MySQL (prices and stock come from the browser).
